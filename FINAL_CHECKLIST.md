## 📋 **DATABASE SCHEMA:**

**Tables: 11**
1. profiles (users)
2. chats (conversations)
3. chat_participants (members)
4. messages (content)
5. message_reactions (emojis)
6. message_status (read receipts)
7. stories (24h content)
8. story_views (who viewed)
9. friendships (connections)
10. calls (history)
11. call_participants (call members)

**Relationships:**
- Users ↔ Profiles (1:1)
- Chats ↔ Participants (1:N)
- Messages ↔ Reactions (1:N)
- Stories ↔ Views (1:N)
- Users ↔ Friendships (M:N)

**Indexes:**
- user_id (all tables)
- chat_id (messages)
- created_at (messages, stories)
- status (friendships, calls)
